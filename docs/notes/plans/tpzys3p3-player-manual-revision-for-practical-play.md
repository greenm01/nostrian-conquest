# Player manual revision for practical play

## Scope and exit

A player-perspective review on 2026-10-13 found that the regular chapters teach
the pieces of the game but not how to play: they follow rules and screens, not
decisions. Chapter 6 (Combat) is the model. The owner approved the revision in
three tasks. Prose uses Dreyer's English in the voice of a late-1980s or early
1990s science-fiction game manual. Exact formulas stay in the appendices. No
rule changes; disputed facts are checked against the code first.

### t147

Quick wins and accuracy fixes: Scorch Earth's capture effect in its own
section; default ROE 6; a short Diplomacy paragraph; chapter 9 fleet advice
matching the destroyer campaign evidence; one name per mission; the menu count
and galaxy-map key; rogue empires defined once; undefended worlds; tax
magnitudes and timing; victory wording; fleet notation and starbase-recovery
procedure moved to appendices.

### t148

Rewrite chapter 3 as "Your First Turns": what an empire starts with, a turn
checklist, and the warnings beginners need, pointing to chapters 4 and 7 for
reference tables.

### t149

Make chapter 9 a strategy chapter (opening builds, colony choice, ROE by job,
tax timing, starbases), cut chapter 7's hostile-mission text to arrival rules
with chapter 6 for effects, and rewrite spec-voiced passages in chapters 4, 5
and 7.

Exit for each: manual builds, revision date current, statements checked against
the appendices and code, PDF read; then an independent Codex review of the
whole revision before publishing.

## Result

Published on 2026-10-13. Chapter 3 is "Your First Turns"; chapter 9 is a
practical strategy chapter; chapter 7 keeps arrival rules and points to chapter
6; spec passages in chapters 4, 5 and 7 moved to Appendices A, B and D, with
Appendix D renamed Interface Reference. Disputed facts were checked against the
code first (menus and keys, mission names, default ROE 6, unarmed guards,
sighting factors, invasion odds, victory, rogue empires, debt, stardock and
army/battery limits); the stale stardock-full claim was also removed from
economics.md.

An independent Codex review (astra) found six must-fix points: tax penalty
versus long-run revenue, surprise needing a prior sighting, split-fire
universals, ROE 10 and the Diplomacy exceptions, the lost blitz escort fact,
and an overreaching stardock statement. All were fixed, along with its smaller
items (first-visit transitions, menu keys, guard-merge and truncation warnings,
mission names in the shared positioning table). A re-check found three
remaining corrections (appendix damage targeting and execution costs, the
colonizer-meets-guard exception, the tax timing phrase), which were applied;
astra then had no remaining objection. The Player Manual (51 pages) and the
Quick Start (5 pages, redated because it shares the positioning table) were
rebuilt and published.
