# Player manual combat chapter for players with rules in an appendix

## Scope and exit

The owner found the player manual's Combat Mechanics chapter too detailed for
players: battles resolve automatically during maintenance. Approved on
2026-10-14.

### t145

Replace the main-body chapter with a short Combat chapter in Dreyer's English:
what a player decides (missions, Rules of Engagement, fleet composition) and
what to expect (who meets whom, damage and recovery, withdrawal, surprise,
attacking worlds, captured worlds, failed assaults). Move the full rules
unchanged into Appendix B, ahead of its existing tables, and fix references.
No rule changes.

Exit: every rule sentence from the old chapter is present in Appendix B; the
manual builds; outline and cross-references read correctly in the PDF.

## Result

The main-body chapter is now **Combat** (about one page): What You Decide,
What to Expect, and Attacking Worlds. Appendix B, renamed **Combat Rules and
Tables**, opens with the full former chapter followed by its existing tables.
A script compared the old chapter with the new appendix: all 208 sentences
survive verbatim except the old heading and five references that now point
within the appendix. The Patrol mission's pointer to Who Fights Whom now names
the appendix. The manual builds at 49 pages; the contents and the new chapter
were read in the PDF. No rule changed.
